The short story

Meet Orelu.

The problem we see. The products we’re building.
A minute or so to get to know us.

Read the video transcript

You answer an enquiry. Copy the details into a sheet. Then chase an approval. By the time you're done, there's another one waiting. That's the everyday work we're looking at. The small, repeated jobs that take people away from running their business. Orelu is our way of working on that problem. We're building a family of software products, each focused on one business need. The approach is simple. Connect the information. Automate the repeated steps. And leave the important decisions with the people who know the business. Our first product is QuotePlate. It's nearly ready, and it's built around how restaurants buy their ingredients. Start with a menu. Review what you need. Then bring suppliers and price requests into the same workflow. Compare the quotes, including tax and delivery. Make a decision, and keep a record of what arrives. We're Utsav and Ria, two founders in India. We're still early, listening to business owners across different industries. We start with one task, clear access rules, and a small pilot. If it helps, we build from there. Got something your team keeps doing the hard way? Tell us. Orelu. Make everyday work easier.
