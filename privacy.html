This page covers Orelu’s company website. QuotePlate is a separate product with its own privacy information.

When you visit

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms or browser storage. Fonts, images and the introduction video are served with the website rather than embedded from third-party services.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, and set essential security cookies to serve and protect the site. These are separate from advertising or analytics tracking.

When you contact us

Email links open your own email application. If you choose to write to us, we receive the information you include and use it to respond to your enquiry. Please do not send passwords, customer records or other confidential business information in an initial enquiry.

When you leave this site

Links to QuotePlate open an external website. That website and your email provider handle information under their own policies.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you have shared with Orelu, or to request its deletion, write to orelu.team@gmail.com.
